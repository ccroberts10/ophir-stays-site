Animas Mountain Loop

A steep, rocky climb to the overlook above the Animas Valley, then a technical descent back to the trailhead. The view from the top is the best in town. Closed each winter for wildlife, so check the dates before you go.

The route

Start at Animas Mountain Trailhead. The loop links Animas Mountain Trail and Animas Mountain Upper, 5.9 miles with about 1,473 feet of climbing, topping out near 8,175 feet.

Load the GPX into Trailforks, Strava or your bike computer to follow it turn by turn. Stay off the trails when they're muddy, and start early in summer to beat the afternoon storms.

Quick answers

How long is Animas Mountain Loop?

5.9 miles with about 1,473 feet of climbing. Most riders take 2.2 to 3.2 hours.

How hard is Animas Mountain Loop?

Advanced. Steep, rocky and technical in places. Best for strong, experienced riders.

Where does Animas Mountain Loop start?

Animas Mountain Trailhead. Use the directions button above for turn-by-turn driving directions to the start.

Can I download the route?

Yes. The GPX file is free and loads into Trailforks, Strava, Garmin, Wahoo and most bike computers.
