Meadow Loop

The right first ride in Durango. Climb Horse Gulch Road out of town, circle the open meadow on smooth, rolling singletrack, and roll back down with town below you. Short enough for kids and a first day at altitude.

The route

Start at Horse Gulch Trailhead. The loop links Horse Gulch Road, Meadow Loop, Cuchillo and Drop Over, 3.7 miles with about 626 feet of climbing, topping out near 7,075 feet.

Load the GPX into Trailforks, Strava or your bike computer to follow it turn by turn. Stay off the trails when they're muddy, and start early in summer to beat the afternoon storms.
