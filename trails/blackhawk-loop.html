San Juan high country · Hermosa Park

Blackhawk Pass Loop

A full backcountry loop from Hermosa Park. Climb the road to Bolam Pass, take the Colorado Trail over Blackhawk Pass, and drop Hotel Draw back to the car. Nearly 26 miles and 3,900 feet, with no shuttle.

Distance25.7 mi
Climbing3,901 ft

Plan this ride with us Directions to the start

The route

Hermosa Park Road up to Bolam Pass, Colorado Trail over Blackhawk Pass, Hotel Draw back down. 25.7 miles, about 3,901 feet up and 3,901 feet down, between 8,854 and 11,992 feet.

A loop, back to the car. No shuttle needed.

Riding up high

  • Start early. Plan to be off the ridges by noon from July through August.
  • Bring a rain shell, a warm layer and more water and food than you think. Weather turns fast above 11,000 feet.
  • Bikes are not allowed in the Weminuche Wilderness. This route stays outside it.
  • New to altitude? Ride something in town on day one.

Quick answers

How long is Blackhawk Pass Loop?

25.7 miles with about 3,901 feet of climbing and 3,901 feet of descending. Most strong riders take 7.7 to 11.2 hours.

When can you ride Blackhawk Pass Loop?

Usually mid-July to early October, once the snow is gone. Start early and be off the ridges by noon; summer afternoons bring lightning above treeline.

How hard is Blackhawk Pass Loop?

Expert. Everything here is above 10,000 feet, so riders coming from sea level should spend a day or two lower first. This is a very long day with few bail-out points.

Does it need a shuttle?

A loop, back to the car. No shuttle needed.
