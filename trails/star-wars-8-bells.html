The route

Start at Overend Mountain Park. The loop links Star Wars, 8 Bells, Spirit, Perins Gulch, Ned's Hill and Ella Vita, 3.9 miles with about 675 feet of climbing, topping out near 7,019 feet.

Load the GPX into Trailforks, Strava or your bike computer to follow it turn by turn. Stay off the trails when they're muddy, and start early in summer to beat the afternoon storms.

Quick answers

How long is Star Wars and 8 Bells?

3.9 miles with about 675 feet of climbing. Most riders take 1.2 to 1.8 hours.

How hard is Star Wars and 8 Bells?

Intermediate. Sustained climbs and some rocks and roots; comfortable for riders who ride singletrack regularly.

Where does Star Wars and 8 Bells start?

Overend Mountain Park. Use the directions button above for turn-by-turn driving directions to the start.

Can I download the route?

Yes. The GPX file is free and loads into Trailforks, Strava, Garmin, Wahoo and most bike computers.
