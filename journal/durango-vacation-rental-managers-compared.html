Durango vacation rental managers, compared

By Casey Roberts · November 2026

Seven companies are the ones a Durango owner is most likely to compare, and only three publish what they charge: Evolve (10% or 15%, with cleaning and repairs left to you), Purgatory Resort’s rental program (the resort keeps 35%) and Ophir Stays (22% for founding homes, 25% after). The others don’t publish a rate. Here is what each one says about itself, with sources, and what to ask before you sign.

We are one of the companies on this list, so read it that way. Every fact about another company comes from its own website or a public listing, linked beside it. Where a company doesn’t publish something, we say so and don’t guess. Checked November 2026.

Vacation Rental Collective (VRC)

A full-service manager with an office at 12 Lizard Head Drive, renting cabins, luxury homes, townhomes and condos in Durango and around Purgatory (Visit Durango listing). In its Marriott Homes & Villas listings it describes itself as locally owned and operated and says every home is inspected before arrival. It also lists on Airbnb and Vrbo. AirROI counts 512 active Airbnb listings under its name for the year to September 2026, a little over half of them in Durango.

It doesn’t publish its owner fee or contract terms. Ask: the rate for your home, what it is charged on, and the notice period.

Purgatory Resort rental program

The resort’s own rental pool for units at the mountain. It publishes its split: owners receive 65% of revenue (Purgatory Lodging Rental Program page). The program includes a front desk that greets guests, in-house maintenance, housekeeping at departure at no added charge to the owner, and group and event bookings through the resort. Its lodging page also lists two free season passes for whole-ownership units.

The page doesn’t say whether anything comes off before the split, or how long the agreement runs. Ask: both, and what restocking costs.

Casago (formerly Vacasa)

Vacasa’s own site says its property management is moving to Casago, its parent company, which runs each market through a franchise owner who lives there. Casago’s Durango page describes full service: housekeeping, maintenance, marketing, guest screening and round-the-clock guest support, with homes listed on its own site and more than 25 booking sites. It says there are no long-term contracts and no hidden fees.

It doesn’t publish the fee. Ask: the rate, and who owns and runs the Durango franchise.

Evolve

A national company that handles the listing, pricing, marketing and bookings and leaves the work at the house to you. You hire the cleaner and the handyman, use Evolve’s vetted local partners, or do it yourself. Fees are published: 10% on the Core plan, 15% on Plus and a custom rate on Pro, plus a one-time $250 onboarding fee, charged only after guests check in (Evolve’s plans page).

The percentage is the lowest here because it covers the least. Ask yourself: what cleaning, inspections and repairs will cost on top, and who checks the house between guests.

Durango Colorado Vacations

A Durango-based manager. Airbtics shows a 4.8 average guest rating on Airbnb and says owner fees are quoted by the home. We couldn’t find published owner terms.

Ask: the rate, what it is charged on, and the notice period.

Red Cliff Properties

A family-owned and operated company at 97 Orchard Lane, offering vacation rentals and property management (Visit Durango listing) and representing more than 50 properties in and around Durango (Durango Magazine directory).

It doesn’t publish its owner fee or contract terms. Ask: the same three questions.

Ophir Stays

Us. Locally owned, founded in 2026 and capped at twenty-five homes. The fee is 22% for the first ten homes, locked for as long as you stay, and 25% after. It covers pricing, guests, cleaning, photography, trip planning for every guest and a monthly report. If you take home less in your first twelve months than in the twelve before, we refund the difference, up to our full fee. Thirty days’ notice to leave, either way. Full owner terms.

What we don’t have yet: guests and reviews. Guest booking opens in 2027, so our first owners are trusting a new company. The guarantee exists for that reason. If a long track record matters most to you, one of the established companies above is the safer choice today.

Managing it yourself

Many Durango owners do. You keep everything but the platform fees and you control the calendar. It costs hours every week and the late calls, and it is hard to do well from out of state. It suits owners who live nearby and enjoy the work.

How to compare them

  1. What is the fee charged on? Nightly rent only, or cleaning and other guest fees too.
  2. What is billed on top? Linens, supplies, card fees, and whether repairs carry a markup.
  3. How do you leave? The notice period, any exit fee, and what happens to booked stays.
  4. Who answers, and where do they live? For you, and for a guest at nine at night.
  5. How many homes does each manager look after?
  6. Can you see a real owner statement? Use our seven-point statement checklist to read it.

Compare on what you keep at the end of the year, not on the percentage. A free estimate from us shows that number for your home: request one.

Questions owners ask

Which Durango vacation rental managers publish their fees?

Three do. Evolve publishes 10% on its Core plan and 15% on Plus. Purgatory Resort publishes a 65% revenue split for owners in its rental program, so the resort keeps 35%. Ophir Stays publishes 22% for its first ten homes and 25% after. Vacation Rental Collective, Casago, Durango Colorado Vacations and Red Cliff Properties do not publish a rate on their public pages.

How much does vacation rental management cost in Durango?

Published rates run from 10% for marketing-only service, where you still pay for cleaning and repairs yourself, to the 35% Purgatory Resort keeps in its rental pool. Airbtics estimates that full-service managers in Durango typically charge 20 to 30%. Always ask what the percentage is charged on and what is billed on top of it.

Is Vacasa still in Durango?

Vacasa says its property management is moving to Casago, its parent company, which runs each market through a local franchise owner. In Durango that business now operates as Casago Durango.

What is the difference between full-service and marketing-only management?

A full-service manager handles the home itself: cleaning, inspections, repairs and guests on the ground. A marketing-only manager, such as Evolve, handles the listing, pricing and bookings and leaves the work at the house to you or to people you hire. Compare the two on total cost, not on the percentage.

Can I switch vacation rental managers in Durango?

Yes. Read the termination clause in your agreement first, then plan the handover around stays already booked. Most switches take 30 to 60 days.
