The trail systems on the map

Durango is one of the few towns where you can ride singletrack from your front door. Each number on the map is a trail system, and each P is a trailhead with parking.

1 Horse Gulch

East of downtown, minutes from Main Avenue. A big web of rolling singletrack through piñon and juniper, with easy and intermediate loops near the trailhead and harder lines farther out. The best first ride in Durango.

Start: Horse Gulch trailhead, at the end of Horse Gulch Road, east of downtown.

Ride it: Meadow Loop · Telegraph and Sidewinder

2 Raider Ridge

The rocky spine above Fort Lewis College. Technical and exposed in places, with views over town and the La Platas the whole way. Best linked with Horse Gulch.

Start: Ride up from Horse Gulch or from the college.

Ride it: Raider Ridge

3 Animas Mountain

The mountain that looks down on north Durango. A steep, rocky climb to big views of the Animas Valley, and a technical descent. It closes each winter for wildlife, so check the dates before you go.

Start: Animas Mountain trailhead, at the north end of West 4th Avenue.

Ride it: Animas Mountain Loop

4 Overend and Perins

Short, punchy trails on the west side of town in Overend Mountain Park, with rock gardens and quick loops. Easy to fit in before dinner.

Start: Overend Mountain Park, west of downtown.

Ride it: Star Wars and 8 Bells · Star Wars and Spirit

5 Twin Buttes

Newer, flowing singletrack west of town below the Twin Buttes, built for riding. A good intermediate day.

Start: Off US 160 west of town.

Ride it: Twin Buttes Loop

6 Colorado Trail

The start, or the finish, of the 500-mile trail to Denver. A long, steady climb up Junction Creek through forest, then back down the same way. As big a day as you want.

Start: Junction Creek trailhead, northwest of town on Junction Creek Road.

7 Log Chutes

Forested loops off Junction Creek Road, higher and cooler than town in summer. Intermediate climbing and a fun, fast descent.

Start: Off Junction Creek Road, past the Colorado Trail trailhead.

Ride it: Log Chutes

8 Carbon Junction

A steep climb out of the river corridor south of town that ties into Horse Gulch. Great for a big loop starting from the Animas River Trail.

Start: South of town near the US 160 and US 550 junction, off the Animas River Trail.

Ride it: Carbon Junction and Skull Rock · Sale Barn and Skull Rock

Before you ride

Stay off trails when they're muddy: ruts last all season. Summer afternoons bring lightning, so start early. Town sits at 6,500 feet and the high points on this map are near 9,000, so drink more than you think and go easy the first day. For turn-by-turn navigation on the trail, use Trailforks or MTB Project.
