Vacation rental management at Cascade Village

By Casey Roberts · November 2026

Cascade Village sits a few minutes north of Purgatory on Highway 550, under the Needle Mountains. Condos here earn most of the year in ski season, with a strong summer behind it.

Can you rent here?

Cascade Village sits near the line between La Plata and San Juan counties, and the county decides which rules and taxes apply. Check your parcel on the assessor's map. San Juan County sets its own rules and taxes, and we confirm the current requirements with the county before we take on a Cascade home. Then the condo association decides whether nightly stays are allowed. Most Cascade Village condos rent nightly today.

What guests come for

  • Ski days at Purgatory, minutes away.
  • The San Juan Skyway north to Silverton and Ouray.
  • Summer hiking, biking and fishing at the edge of the Weminuche.
  • Quiet: no resort base crowds outside the door.

How we run a home here

  • The walk cleared and heat checked before every winter arrival.
  • Heat tape and pipes checked in every cold snap, occupied or not.
  • Rates moved daily around holidays, spring break and powder forecasts.
  • A deep clean and a repair report every mud season.
