Questions

Ophir Stays, in short.

What is Ophir Stays?

Ophir Stays is a locally owned vacation rental management company in Durango, Colorado, founded by Casey Roberts, who has lived here since 2003. It manages a private collection capped at twenty-five homes and plans each guest's trip. About Ophir.

How much does Ophir Stays charge to manage a Durango vacation rental?

The management fee is 22% for the first ten founding homes, locked for as long as the owner stays, and 25% after that. It covers pricing, guests, cleaning, photography, trip planning and a monthly report. It costs $0 to join, and either side can leave with thirty days' notice. See what owners get.

What is the first-year guarantee?

If an owner takes home less in the first twelve months with Ophir than in the twelve months before, Ophir refunds the difference, up to the full management fee it earned that year. Homes need at least twelve months of rental history to qualify. How the guarantee works.

Where does Ophir Stays manage homes?

In Durango, the Animas Valley, Hermosa and the Purgatory area, including Cascade Village, wherever nightly rentals are allowed. Every home is within 45 minutes of town or Purgatory.

How is Ophir different from a large vacation rental manager?

Ophir is one local manager with no more than twenty-five homes. Owners and guests text Casey directly from 7am to 10pm, repairs are billed at cost with no markup, there is no long contract, and every guest stay comes with trip planning.

What could my Durango home earn as a vacation rental?

Ask for a free estimate. Within 24 hours you get a three-page report with expected rent month by month, what you keep after every cost, and comparable homes. Casey checks every number himself. Request an estimate, or read what a Durango vacation home earns.
