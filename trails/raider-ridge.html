The route

Start at Horse Gulch Trailhead. The loop links Half Ridge, Extended Ridge, Hyper-Extended Ridge, Sugar and Horse Gulch Road, 8 miles with about 1,364 feet of climbing, topping out near 7,879 feet.

Load the GPX into Trailforks, Strava or your bike computer to follow it turn by turn. Stay off the trails when they're muddy, and start early in summer to beat the afternoon storms.
