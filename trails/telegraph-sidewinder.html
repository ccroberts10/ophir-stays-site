The classic Horse Gulch loop. A long, steady climb on Telegraph, then Sidewinder and the mesa trails back toward town. Flowing, fast, and the ride most locals do after work.

The route

Start at Horse Gulch Trailhead. The loop links Telegraph, Sidewinder, Manilla Vanilla, Mesa Connector and Horse Gulch Road, 8.6 miles with about 1,007 feet of climbing, topping out near 7,535 feet.

Load the GPX into Trailforks, Strava or your bike computer to follow it turn by turn. Stay off the trails when they're muddy, and start early in summer to beat the afternoon storms.
