The route

Start at Twin Buttes, off US 160 west. The loop links Twin Buttes, Upper Twin Buttes, Upper Ed & Flo, Ed & Flo and Lightner Creek, 6.4 miles with about 815 feet of climbing, topping out near 7,451 feet.

Load the GPX into Trailforks, Strava or your bike computer to follow it turn by turn. Stay off the trails when they're muddy, and start early in summer to beat the afternoon storms.

Quick answers

How long is Twin Buttes Loop?

6.4 miles with about 815 feet of climbing. Most riders take 1.8 to 2.6 hours.

How hard is Twin Buttes Loop?

Intermediate. Sustained climbs and some rocks and roots; comfortable for riders who ride singletrack regularly.

Where does Twin Buttes Loop start?

Twin Buttes, off US 160 west. Use the directions button above for turn-by-turn driving directions to the start.

Can I download the route?

Yes. The GPX file is free and loads into Trailforks, Strava, Garmin, Wahoo and most bike computers.
