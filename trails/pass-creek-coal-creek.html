San Juan high country · Coal Bank Pass

Pass Creek to Coal Creek

The best short ride in the high country. Climb Pass Creek from Coal Bank Pass to the meadows under Engineer Mountain, cross on the Engineer Mountain Trail, then drop Coal Creek and spin a short stretch of US 550 back to the car. No shuttle needed.

Distance7.8 mi
Climbing1,714 ft
High point11,884 ft
ShuttleNo, loop

Plan this ride with us Directions to the start

The route

Loop: Pass Creek, Engineer Mountain Trail, Coal Creek, a short ride back up US 550. 7.8 miles, about 1,714 feet up and 1,723 feet down, between 10,284 and 11,884 feet.

A loop, back to the car. No shuttle needed.

Riding up high

  • Start early. Plan to be off the ridges by noon from July through August.
  • Bring a rain shell, a warm layer and more water and food than you think. Weather turns fast above 11,000 feet.
  • Bikes are not allowed in the Weminuche Wilderness. This route stays outside it.
  • New to altitude? Ride something in town on day one.

Quick answers

How long is Pass Creek to Coal Creek?

7.8 miles with about 1,714 feet of climbing and 1,723 feet of descending. Most strong riders take 2.7 to 3.9 hours.

When can you ride Pass Creek to Coal Creek?

Usually mid-July to early October, once the snow is gone. Start early and be off the ridges by noon; summer afternoons bring lightning above treeline.

How hard is Pass Creek to Coal Creek?

Advanced. Everything here is above 10,000 feet, so riders coming from sea level should spend a day or two lower first.

Does it need a shuttle?

A loop, back to the car. No shuttle needed.
