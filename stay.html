The details

How a stay unfolds

  1. 30 days before

    Tell us what you love

    Skiing, riding, the river, a slow morning with good coffee. A short form in the app, or a call with your host.

  2. 14 days before

    Sizes and lists

    Heights, boot sizes and riding styles for everyone in the party. Your grocery list, dietary needs and how you take your coffee.

  3. 7 days before

    Your itinerary

    Every day planned and built around the forecast: tickets, tee times, guides and tables confirmed.

  4. Arrival day

    Walk in

    The fire is laid and the fridge is stocked. Skis, boots and bikes wait in the gear room, each labeled with a name. Your door code arrives at four.

  5. During your stay

    We adjust

    Storms coming in? Your ride moves to tomorrow and we suggest the hot springs. Text us anything, any time.

  6. After you leave

    We remember

    Your sizes, your coffee and your favorite table, saved for next time.

Questions

Before you come.

What is included with every Ophir stay?

A home inspected before every arrival, hotel-grade linens, robes and toiletries, a welcome kit of local coffee and chocolate, your door code and house guide in the app, weather-aware ideas for every day, and a local host by text from 7am to 10pm.

How much does trip planning cost?

The Arranged Stay is $395 per stay for up to six guests and $595 for groups of seven to twelve. The Private Stay, with a dedicated host, is $1,950 for up to five nights and $295 for each additional night.

Do package prices include rentals, lift tickets and guides?

No. Package prices cover our planning, booking and hosting. Rentals, lift tickets, guides, chefs, massage, transport and groceries are paid separately at our partners' rates, with no markup. You see and confirm every price in the app before anything is charged.

Can skis, boots and bikes be waiting at the house?

Yes. With the Arranged Stay, skis and boots are sized to each guest and bikes are fitted and delivered before you arrive. On any other stay it is a single add-on: $95 for up to four guests and $20 each after, plus the rental.

What happens if the weather changes?

Your itinerary is built around the forecast and moves with it. If a storm is coming in, your ride moves to tomorrow and we suggest something else for today.

How do I reach someone during my stay?

Text your local host from 7am to 10pm. Urgent issues get a response at any hour.

When can I book?

Guest booking opens in 2027. Email us your dates and we will hold the first look at our homes for you.
