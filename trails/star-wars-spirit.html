The longer Overend loop. Spirit adds more climbing and more of the park, with the same rocky, playful riding as Star Wars.

The route

Start at Overend Mountain Park. The loop links Spirit, Star Wars, Perins Gulch, Ned's Hill, Ella Vita and Tech Cutoff, 6.2 miles with about 966 feet of climbing, topping out near 7,019 feet.

Load the GPX into Trailforks, Strava or your bike computer to follow it turn by turn. Stay off the trails when they're muddy, and start early in summer to beat the afternoon storms.
