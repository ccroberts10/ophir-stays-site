San Juan high country · Coal Bank Pass

Deer Creek

Up Pass Creek and across the Engineer Mountain Trail to the highest point of the day near 11,900 feet, then down Deer Creek to US 550. A quieter descent with a short shuttle back to Coal Bank.

Distance8.6 mi
Climbing1,558 ft
High point11,931 ft
ShuttleYes

Plan this ride with us Directions to the start

The route

Pass Creek, Engineer Mountain Trail, Deer Creek down to US 550. 8.6 miles, about 1,558 feet up and 2,323 feet down, between 9,838 and 11,931 feet.

Point to point. We book the shuttle with a licensed local company: drop at Pass Creek Trailhead, Coal Bank Pass, pickup at the finish.

Riding up high

  • Start early. Plan to be off the ridges by noon from July through August.
  • Bring a rain shell, a warm layer and more water and food than you think. Weather turns fast above 11,000 feet.
  • Bikes are not allowed in the Weminuche Wilderness. This route stays outside it.
  • New to altitude? Ride something in town on day one.

Quick answers

How long is Deer Creek?

8.6 miles with about 1,558 feet of climbing and 2,323 feet of descending. Most strong riders take 2.8 to 4.0 hours.

When can you ride Deer Creek?

Usually mid-July to early October, once the snow is gone. Start early and be off the ridges by noon; summer afternoons bring lightning above treeline.

How hard is Deer Creek?

Advanced. Everything here is above 10,000 feet, so riders coming from sea level should spend a day or two lower first.

Does it need a shuttle?

Point to point. We book the shuttle with a licensed local company: drop at Pass Creek Trailhead, Coal Bank Pass, pickup at the finish.
