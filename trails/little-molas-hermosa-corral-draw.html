Colorado Trail: Little Molas to Hermosa

The big one. Fifty-three miles of the Colorado Trail from Little Molas Lake over Rolling Mountain and Bolam Pass, down Corral Draw and out Hermosa Creek. A full day for very fit riders, or two with support.

The route

Colorado Trail over Rolling Mountain and Bolam Pass, down Corral Draw, out Hermosa Creek. 52.7 miles, about 6,675 feet up and 9,850 feet down, between 7,470 and 12,494 feet.

Point to point. We book the shuttle with a licensed local company: drop at Little Molas Lake Trailhead, pickup at the finish.

Riding up high

  • Start early. Plan to be off the ridges by noon from July through August.
  • Bring a rain shell, a warm layer and more water and food than you think. Weather turns fast above 11,000 feet.
  • Bikes are not allowed in the Weminuche Wilderness. This route stays outside it.
  • New to altitude? Ride something in town on day one.

Quick answers

How long is Colorado Trail: Little Molas to Hermosa?

52.7 miles with about 6,675 feet of climbing and 9,850 feet of descending. Most strong riders take 15.0 to 21.7 hours.

When can you ride Colorado Trail: Little Molas to Hermosa?

Usually mid-July to early October, once the snow is gone. Start early and be off the ridges by noon; summer afternoons bring lightning above treeline.

How hard is Colorado Trail: Little Molas to Hermosa?

Expert. Everything here is above 10,000 feet, so riders coming from sea level should spend a day or two lower first. This is a very long day with few bail-out points.

Does it need a shuttle?

Point to point. We book the shuttle with a licensed local company: drop at Little Molas Lake Trailhead, pickup at the finish.
