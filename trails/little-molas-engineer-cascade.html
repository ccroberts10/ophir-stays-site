San Juan high country · Molas Pass

Little Molas to Engineer Mountain

The high traverse. From Little Molas Lake the Colorado Trail stays above 11,000 feet for miles, tops out over 12,000, then joins the full Engineer Mountain Trail down to Cascade. Shuttled to Molas Pass.

Distance17.9 mi
Climbing2,449 ft
High point12,190 ft
ShuttleYes

Plan this ride with us Directions to the start

The route

Colorado Trail west from Little Molas, then the full Engineer Mountain Trail to Cascade. 17.9 miles, about 2,449 feet up and 4,291 feet down, between 9,064 and 12,190 feet.

Point to point. We book the shuttle with a licensed local company: drop at Little Molas Lake Trailhead, pickup at the finish.

Riding up high

  • Start early. Plan to be off the ridges by noon from July through August.
  • Bring a rain shell, a warm layer and more water and food than you think. Weather turns fast above 11,000 feet.
  • Bikes are not allowed in the Weminuche Wilderness. This route stays outside it.
  • New to altitude? Ride something in town on day one.

Quick answers

How long is Little Molas to Engineer Mountain?

17.9 miles with about 2,449 feet of climbing and 4,291 feet of descending. Most strong riders take 5.2 to 7.6 hours.

When can you ride Little Molas to Engineer Mountain?

Usually mid-July to early October, once the snow is gone. Start early and be off the ridges by noon; summer afternoons bring lightning above treeline.

How hard is Little Molas to Engineer Mountain?

Advanced. Everything here is above 10,000 feet, so riders coming from sea level should spend a day or two lower first.

Does it need a shuttle?

Point to point. We book the shuttle with a licensed local company: drop at Little Molas Lake Trailhead, pickup at the finish.
