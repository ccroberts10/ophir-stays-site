Eat

Where to eat in Durango, from a local

By Casey Roberts · October 2026

I bartended at some of Durango’s best restaurants before PA school, and I’ve spent twenty-three years eating my way down Main Avenue. These are the thirteen places I send friends, sorted by the night you’re having.

A note on timing: summer weekends and Christmas week fill early everywhere on this list. Book a week or more ahead, or ask us and we’ll hold the table.

A night to remember

Eolus Bar & Dining · 919 Main Ave
New American cooking and one of the few covered rooftops downtown, with the mountains in view. The pick for an anniversary. Book ahead.

Serafina's Steakhouse · 144 E 9th St
The newest serious steakhouse in town, from the people behind Chimayo. A player piano, an intimate bar, and steak and seafood done properly. Open Thursday to Monday; reserve by phone.

636 Main Ave · 636 Main Ave
The old Ken & Sue's, still one of the most reliable dinners on Main. Contemporary American with Asian touches and a good patio. Dinner only; book on Tock.

Date night

Perbacco Cucina Italiana · 505 Main Ave
Classic pastas and risotto in a warm room on lower Main. Quiet enough to talk, good enough to come back. Reserve on OpenTable.

PRIMI Pasta & Wine Bar · 1201 Main Ave
Small, handmade pasta and an Italian wine list on north Main, with a patio when it’s warm. Call ahead; it fills up.

El Moro Spirits & Tavern · 945 Main Ave
Serious cocktails in a historic saloon building, with a gastropub menu to match. Book on weekends. Weekend brunch is worth knowing about.

Out with friends

East by Southwest · 160 E College Dr
Sushi and Pan-Asian plates, a sake lounge and a strong happy hour. The best option in town when someone at the table eats vegetarian or vegan.

Chimayo Stone Fired Kitchen · 862 Main Ave
Stone-fired ovens, a long bar, a patio and a chef’s counter if you like to watch the kitchen. Lively without being loud.

With kids

Fired Up Pizzeria · 735 Main Ave
Wood-fired Neapolitan pizza in a big room that handles families easily, with a rooftop patio in summer. Lunch is the calm time.

Carver Brewing Co. · 1022 Main Ave
Durango’s original brewery, pouring since 1988. Beer garden, a menu for everyone, open from breakfast to dinner. Walk in.

Steamworks Brewing Co. · 801 E 2nd Ave
Big, busy and happy to seat a group of twelve with three kids under ten. Brewpub food, a patio and parking.

Quick and good

Zia Taqueria · 2977 Main Ave and 400 S Camino del Rio
Counter service, burritos and tacos made from scratch, fast and cheap. A kids’ menu and two locations.

RGP's Flame Grilled Wraps · 835 Main Ave
Flame-grilled wraps at a weekday lunch counter downtown. Closed weekends, and not a dinner spot, but a great lunch.
