Twin Buttes Loop

Purpose-built flow trails below the Twin Buttes, west of town. Smooth climbing, berms on the way down, and big views of Perins Peak.

Distance6.4 mi
Climbing815 ft
High point7,451 ft
DifficultyIntermediate

Download GPX Directions to the start

The route

Start at Twin Buttes, off US 160 west. The loop links Twin Buttes, Upper Twin Buttes, Upper Ed & Flo, Ed & Flo and Lightner Creek, 6.4 miles with about 815 feet of climbing, topping out near 7,451 feet.

Load the GPX into Trailforks, Strava or your bike computer to follow it turn by turn. Stay off the trails when they're muddy, and start early in summer to beat the afternoon storms.
