How it works

Four steps to your first guest.

  1. Estimate

    Tell us about your home. Within two days you get a personal income estimate built from real Durango booking data.

  2. Walkthrough

    We meet you at the house, or on a video call if you're out of state, and go through the standard together.

  3. Ready

    Linens, photography, smart lock, gear room. We handle every item on the list and send you one bill.

  4. Live

    Your home goes live. Every month you get a report: income, reviews, photos after each clean, and anything we fixed.

The guarantee

We beat your last year, or we refund the difference.

If your home earns less in its first twelve months with Ophir than in the twelve months before, we refund the difference, up to the full management fee we earned that year.

Who qualifiesHomes with at least twelve months of rental history we can see: Airbnb, VRBO or prior manager statements.
Your baselineGross booking revenue for the twelve months before you sign. Nightly rent only: cleaning fees, pet fees and taxes are excluded from both years.
Your own staysNights you block for yourself lower the baseline by that year's average nightly rate.
PricingWe set nightly rates and minimum stays. Nights you hold above our rate aren't covered.
Not coveredClosures no one controls: wildfire or road closures, government orders, damage that keeps the home offline more than seven days.
How it paysWe compare at month twelve and pay within thirty days.
LeavingThirty days' notice, either way, no penalty.

Summary only. Full terms are in the owner agreement.

Our promises

What we put in writing.

i.

Damage, covered

Every guest pays a damage waiver. It covers your home up to $2,500 a stay, and you never file a claim.

ii.

Small fixes, included

Bulbs, batteries, filters, a loose handle: fixed and never billed. Bigger repairs are billed at cost plus 10%, with photos, and nothing over $300 happens without your yes.

iii.

Fifteen minutes

Guests and owners hear back within fifteen minutes, 7am to 10pm. Someone is at your house within thirty minutes, any hour.

iv.

Priced every day

Nightly rates move daily with the season, events and demand, set by live Durango data and checked by someone who lives here.

The standard

What every home in the Collection has.

If your home falls short, we send you the list of what to fix. Often it's a weekend's work, and we can arrange it.

  • Professional photography
  • Hotel-grade mattresses and white linens
  • A hot tub or a real view
  • A gear room: bikes, skis, boot dryers
  • Fast Wi-Fi and a smart lock
  • A kitchen stocked for a real dinner
  • No deferred maintenance
  • Within 45 minutes of town or Purgatory
  • Permitted for short-term rental

Questions

Before you apply.

I already have a manager. Can I switch?

Yes. Most agreements need thirty to sixty days' notice. We handle the handover and move your future bookings across.

Can I still use my home?

Of course. Block your dates in the app any time. Your own nights simply lower the guarantee baseline, so the comparison stays fair.

Where do you list my home?

On Airbnb and VRBO, and on ophirstays.com, where returning guests book direct. Direct bookings skip platform fees, which means more for you.

Who cleans and maintains it?

Local crews we've known for years, inspected after every clean. Small fixes are included. Nothing over $300 happens without your approval.

Why only twenty-five homes?

Because the service only works if we know every home personally. When the Collection is full, new owners join a waiting list.
