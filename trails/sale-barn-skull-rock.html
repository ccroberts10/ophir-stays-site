Sale Barn and Skull Rock

Rolling mesa riding south of town, with long views and fewer people than Horse Gulch. Dry early in spring when higher trails are still snowed in.

Distance7.2 mi
Climbing848 ft
High point7,177 ft
DifficultyIntermediate

Download GPX Directions to the start

The route

Start at Sale Barn Trailhead. The loop links Sale Barn, Skull Rock, South Rim, Big Canyon and Cowboy Upper, 7.2 miles with about 848 feet of climbing, topping out near 7,177 feet.

Load the GPX into Trailforks, Strava or your bike computer to follow it turn by turn. Stay off the trails when they're muddy, and start early in summer to beat the afternoon storms.
