The route

Start at Log Chutes Trailhead, Junction Creek Road. The loop links Log Chutes 1 and Log Chutes 2, 5.9 miles with about 654 feet of climbing, topping out near 8,043 feet.

Load the GPX into Trailforks, Strava or your bike computer to follow it turn by turn. Stay off the trails when they're muddy, and start early in summer to beat the afternoon storms.

Quick answers

How long is Log Chutes?

5.9 miles with about 654 feet of climbing. Most riders take 1.6 to 2.3 hours.

How hard is Log Chutes?

Intermediate. Sustained climbs and some rocks and roots; comfortable for riders who ride singletrack regularly.

Where does Log Chutes start?

Log Chutes Trailhead, Junction Creek Road. Use the directions button above for turn-by-turn driving directions to the start.

Can I download the route?

Yes. The GPX file is free and loads into Trailforks, Strava, Garmin, Wahoo and most bike computers.
