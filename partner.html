Questions

The fine print, in plain words.

What does it cost me?

Nothing. There is no fee and no contract. Leave any time.

Does anything change about how I run my listing?

No. You keep your listing, your calendar, your cleaner and your pricing. Ophir never touches the house.

Will you market to my guests or try to book them elsewhere?

Never. We only contact your guests about the trip they've already booked with you. We will not invite them to book another home or book direct. That's in writing in our partner agreement.

What do I earn?

10% of every Ophir package and add-on your guests buy, such as The Arranged Stay or gear fitted and delivered. Rentals, lift tickets and guides are billed at the shop's own rate with no markup, so they're not included. You get a statement and a payment each month.

Who handles questions about the house?

You do, as always. Your guests' trip page tells them to message you on Airbnb or Vrbo for anything about the home. We handle the trip.

Where does the link go?

In your check-in message, your house guide or on the card we give you for the counter. Airbnb and Vrbo don't allow outside links in the listing itself, so keep it to messages sent after booking.
