Engineer Mountain Trail from Pass Creek

Climb Pass Creek to the shoulder of Engineer Mountain, then ride the Engineer Mountain Trail almost 2,700 feet down through meadow and spruce to Cascade. Short on climbing and long on descending, with a shuttle back up the pass.

Distance8.2 mi
Climbing1,083 ft
High point11,731 ft
ShuttleYes

Plan this ride with us Directions to the start

The route

Pass Creek up, Engineer Mountain Trail down to Cascade. 8.2 miles, about 1,083 feet up and 2,658 feet down, between 9,064 and 11,731 feet.

Point to point. We book the shuttle with a licensed local company: drop at Pass Creek Trailhead, Coal Bank Pass, pickup at the finish.

Riding up high

  • Start early. Plan to be off the ridges by noon from July through August.
  • Bring a rain shell, a warm layer and more water and food than you think. Weather turns fast above 11,000 feet.
  • Bikes are not allowed in the Weminuche Wilderness. This route stays outside it.
  • New to altitude? Ride something in town on day one.

Quick answers

How long is Engineer Mountain Trail from Pass Creek?

8.2 miles with about 1,083 feet of climbing and 2,658 feet of descending. Most strong riders take 2.4 to 3.4 hours.

When can you ride Engineer Mountain Trail from Pass Creek?

Usually mid-July to early October, once the snow is gone. Start early and be off the ridges by noon; summer afternoons bring lightning above treeline.

How hard is Engineer Mountain Trail from Pass Creek?

Advanced. Everything here is above 10,000 feet, so riders coming from sea level should spend a day or two lower first.

Does it need a shuttle?

Point to point. We book the shuttle with a licensed local company: drop at Pass Creek Trailhead, Coal Bank Pass, pickup at the finish.
