Who Ophir is for

A good fit, and not.

Ophir is a good fit if

  • You want one person to call, and you want that person to live in Durango.
  • Your home is in town, the Animas Valley, Hermosa or near Purgatory, and nightly rentals are allowed there.
  • You care how guests are treated as much as what they pay.
  • You want the quiet weeks on your calendar working harder.

Ophir is probably not for you if

  • You are looking for the lowest fee in town and nothing else.
  • You want to approve every booking yourself.
  • You would rather not put anything back into the home: photos, linens, repairs.
  • You need a manager with hundreds of homes and a call center. We will never be that.
