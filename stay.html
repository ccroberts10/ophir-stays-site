The details

How a stay unfolds

  1. 30 days before

    Tell us what you love

    Skiing, riding, the river, a slow morning with good coffee. A short form in the app, or a call with your host.

  2. 14 days before

    Sizes and lists

    Heights, boot sizes and riding styles for everyone in the party. Your grocery list, dietary needs and how you take your coffee.

  3. 7 days before

    Your itinerary

    Every day planned and built around the forecast: tickets, tee times, guides and tables confirmed.

  4. Arrival day

    Walk in

    The fire is laid and the fridge is stocked. Skis, boots and bikes wait in the gear room, each labeled with a name. Your door code arrives at four.

  5. During your stay

    We adjust

    Storms coming in? Your ride moves to tomorrow and we suggest the hot springs. Text us anything, any time.

  6. After you leave

    We remember

    Your sizes, your coffee and your favorite table, saved for next time.
