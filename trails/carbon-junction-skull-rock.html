A steep climb out of the river corridor south of town that ties into Skull Rock and the Horse Gulch network. A big, hilly loop with views back up the Animas Valley.

The route

Start at Carbon Junction Trailhead. The loop links Carbon Junction, Skull Rock, Big Canyon, Sidewinder and Cowboy Upper, 8.7 miles with about 1,167 feet of climbing, topping out near 7,178 feet.

Load the GPX into Trailforks, Strava or your bike computer to follow it turn by turn. Stay off the trails when they're muddy, and start early in summer to beat the afternoon storms.

Quick answers

How long is Carbon Junction and Skull Rock?

8.7 miles with about 1,167 feet of climbing. Most riders take 2.5 to 3.7 hours.

How hard is Carbon Junction and Skull Rock?

Advanced. Steep, rocky and technical in places. Best for strong, experienced riders.

Where does Carbon Junction and Skull Rock start?

Carbon Junction Trailhead. Use the directions button above for turn-by-turn driving directions to the start.

Can I download the route?

Yes. The GPX file is free and loads into Trailforks, Strava, Garmin, Wahoo and most bike computers.
