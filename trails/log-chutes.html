Log Chutes

Forested loops off Junction Creek Road, a thousand feet higher and cooler than town. Pine needles, steady climbing and a fast descent. The pick for a hot summer afternoon.

Distance5.9 mi
Climbing654 ft
High point8,043 ft
DifficultyIntermediate

Download GPX Directions to the start

The route

Start at Log Chutes Trailhead, Junction Creek Road. The loop links Log Chutes 1 and Log Chutes 2, 5.9 miles with about 654 feet of climbing, topping out near 8,043 feet.

Load the GPX into Trailforks, Strava or your bike computer to follow it turn by turn. Stay off the trails when they're muddy, and start early in summer to beat the afternoon storms.
