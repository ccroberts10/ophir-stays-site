What Basecamp handles

Every day planned around your riders.

  • Routes matched to each rider, with GPX files before you arrive
  • Shuttles booked with licensed local shuttle companies
  • Tailwind and recovery basics stocked for every rider
  • Bikes or skis fitted and delivered, mechanical help the same day
  • A conditions text by 7am, and plans that move with the weather
  • If someone gets hurt, we get the bike to the shop and rearrange the rest of the trip

Basecamp homes have a garage or locked bike storage, a wash station and a gear room, in town, the Animas Valley or up toward Purgatory.

A sample week

Five days in July.

  1. Day 1 Arrive, spin Telegraph and Sidewinder in town. 8.6 miles at 6,600 feet to settle in.
  2. Day 2 Pass Creek to Coal Creek from Coal Bank Pass. 7.8 miles, 1,700 feet, back to the car.
  3. Day 3 Engine Creek. 13 miles, mostly down, shuttle back from Cascade.
  4. Day 4 Rest. The river, a massage, an early dinner.
  5. Day 5 Little Molas to Engineer Mountain. 18 miles, 12,190 feet high, shuttled to Molas Pass.

Every plan moves with the forecast and the group. Riders new to altitude start lower.
